# Frontend Design

Approach this as the design lead at a design studio known for giving every client a distinct visual identity that is not mistaken for anyone else's. Avoid cliché or templated proposals. Make deliberate, opinionated choices about palette, typography, and layout that are specific to this brief, and take aesthetic risk if justified.

## Ground your designs in the subject matter
- Identify the product, audience, and primary job before designing.
- Ground visual choices in the subject matter's industry, materials, and vernacular (e.g. water resource analytics, hydrology, telemetry vs commercial consumer apps).
- Build with the brief's real content and subject matter throughout.

## Design principles
- **Intentional Hero Section:** Open with the most characteristic element in the subject's world (telemetry balance, live interactive radar, geospatial crisis matrix).
- **Typography with Character:** Choose typefaces deliberately with clear hierarchy, weights, and spacing. Default to line lengths under 80 characters.
- **Avoid cliché AI tells:** Avoid accenting single random words with gradients, avoid unnecessary labels over everything, and avoid generic numbered badges unless representing a true sequence.
- **Visual Structure is Information:** Outlines, dividers, grids, and status badges should encode functional data rather than mere decorative fluff.
- **Meaningful Motion:** Use motion that answers user actions (expanding cards, filter toggles, live simulation recalculations) rather than constant distracting ambient animations.

## Aesthetic Direction for JalRakshak (Hydrological Intelligence & Water Crisis Dashboard)
- **Palette:** Deep ocean/slate navy canvas (`#0b132b`, `#1c2541`), vivid water cyan accents (`#00f0ff`, `#3a86ff`), amber warnings (`#f59e0b`), and crimson emergency crisis indicators (`#ef4444`).
- **Typography:** Modern technical sans-serif for numbers/data readout combined with clean editorial sans for headers.
- **Components:** High-contrast telemetry cards, ECharts spatial water balance curves, Non-Revenue Water loss radars, and real-time interactive What-If simulation sliders.
